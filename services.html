Services

Interior Design Services in Hyderabad for Homes, Hotels, Offices & Cafés

Based in Kondapur, Hyderabad, Studio Naomikaa provides planning-first interior design for homes, apartments, hotels, offices, cafés and retail spaces across India. Whatever the brief, the approach is the same: understand how the space will be used, plan for it, then design and build around that plan.

Space type 01

Residential Interiors

Homes and apartments planned around your household — storage that matches how you actually keep things, layouts that work for daily routines, and finishes chosen to age well, not just photograph well.

  • Full-home and single-room interior design
  • Space planning and furniture layout
  • Modular kitchens, wardrobes and storage design
  • Lighting design and material specification
  • Turnkey execution with site supervision
Discuss a residential project
Space type 02

Hotel Interiors

Guest rooms, corridors, lobbies and back-of-house areas planned for comfort, brand identity, and the realities of daily housekeeping and maintenance — from a single refurbished floor to a full new build.

  • Guest room and suite layout planning
  • Lobby, restaurant and common-area design
  • Housekeeping-friendly material and finish selection
  • FF&E (furniture, fixtures & equipment) specification
  • Phased execution to minimise disruption to operations
Discuss a hotel project
Space type 03

Office Interiors

Workspaces that balance focus areas, collaboration zones and meeting spaces, built to reflect your brand without turning into an empty showroom that nobody actually uses.

  • Workspace planning and headcount-flexible layouts
  • Meeting rooms, breakout zones and reception design
  • Acoustic, lighting and ergonomic considerations
  • Brand-led material and colour direction
  • Vendor coordination and fit-out management
Discuss an office project
Space type 04

Café & Retail

Seating plans, counter flow and ambience designed to work under a full service rush, not just at a quiet photoshoot — because a café interior earns its keep in covers per hour.

  • Seating layout and covers optimisation
  • Counter, kitchen and back-of-house flow planning
  • Signature lighting and material atmosphere
  • Retail fixture and display design
  • Fast-track execution for opening deadlines
