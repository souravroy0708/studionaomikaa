How we work

Five steps, the same order, every time.

01

Discover

Site visit, measurements and a conversation about how you'll actually use the space.

02

Plan

Layout, circulation and storage resolved on paper before any styling begins.

03

Design

Materials, lighting and furniture selected to support the plan, not distract from it.

04

Specify

Detailed drawings and a fixed costing sheet, so there are no surprises mid-project.

05

Execute

Contractor coordination and site supervision through to final handover.
