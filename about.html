About the studio

Studio Naomikaa — Interior Design Studio Founded by Brishti Roy

Studio Naomikaa is a Hyderabad-based interior design studio founded by Brishti Roy. The studio is named after her daughter, Naomikaa, and grew from a long-held desire to pursue architecture and interior design as a profession — with planning a space properly before decorating it at the centre of the approach.

Our approach

Plan the space. Then design what fills it.

Most interior projects go wrong before a single material is chosen — in a layout that fights how people actually move, store things and gather. Studio Naomikaa treats the floor plan as the real design decision, and everything after it — material, lighting, furniture — as detailing that supports the plan.

That discipline is why the studio is trusted with work that has to perform under pressure: hotel rooms that housekeeping can service quickly, offices that survive a headcount change, cafés that hold a Saturday rush without a bottleneck at the counter.

What we believe

Three principles behind every project.

Plan before palette

Layout, circulation and storage are resolved before a single finish is selected — so the design still works after the styling shoot ends.

Built for daily use

Materials and details are chosen for how they wear over years of real use, not just how they photograph on handover day.

One point of accountability

From the first drawing to the final punch list, one team stays responsible — no handoffs between "designer" and "contractor."

Our story

How Studio Naomikaa got its name.

Jan 2024

Naomikaa is born

Brishti's daughter, Naomikaa, is born on 4 January 2024 — and becomes the reason everything after this changes.

2024–25

A long-held desire, finally acted on

Inspired by Naomikaa, Brishti decides to stop putting off a long-standing desire to work in architecture and interior design, and starts planning a studio of her own.

2026

Studio Naomikaa launches

The studio opens, named after the person who inspired it — designing homes, hotels, offices and cafés with the same care that went into choosing its name.
